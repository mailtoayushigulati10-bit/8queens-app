How to Play the 8-Queens Game

The goal of this game is to place 8 queens on an 8x8 chessboard so that no two queens attack each other.

  • Queens can move horizontally, vertically, and diagonally across the board.
  • No two queens should share the same row.
  • No two queens should share the same column.
  • No two queens should share the same diagonal.
  • Click on a square to place or remove a queen.
  • Use the "Reset" button to clear the board at any time.

Try placing the queens carefully and see if you can solve it yourself! You can also view the optimal solution for guidance.
